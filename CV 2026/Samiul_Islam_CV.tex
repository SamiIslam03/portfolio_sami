\section{Research Experience}

\noindent\textbf{IoT and LLM-Based Bangla Sign Language Recognition with a Smart Glove} \hfill Ongoing\\
\textit{M.Sc. Thesis}, CPI Lab, IAT, BUET (Supervisor: Tahsina Farah Sanam, Ph.D.)\\
$\bullet$ Built a sensor glove with four flex sensors and an MPU-6050 motion sensor; recorded 1,369 samples of 23 Bangla Sign Language signs from one signer, 12 of them mental-health terms\\
$\bullet$ A 1D CNN recognized signs with 97.2\% accuracy in 0.37~ms per sign; on unseen recording sessions macro-F1 fell to 0.733, and the motion sensors alone generalized better than the full glove\\
$\bullet$ An offline language model turns recognized signs into a sentence, spoken only after the user confirms; first-author manuscript almost ready to submit\\
$\bullet$ Next version (in progress): a lighter glove with six motion sensors and no flex sensors, built on comfortable gaming gloves that fit any hand size and redesigned after user-experience research; HCI studies under way

\vspace{6pt}
\noindent\textbf{SMMNet-FedHE: Privacy-Preserving Framework for Stereotypical Motor Movement Monitoring with WiFi CSI} \hfill Accepted, IEEE TENCON 2026\\
\textit{CPI Lab, IAT, BUET}, with A. Islam and T. F. Sanam\\
$\bullet$ Combined federated learning and homomorphic encryption for privacy-preserving, contactless detection of stereotypical motor movements for early autism screening\\
$\bullet$ Extracted multi-domain features from WiFi CSI data; accuracy above 80\% across centralized and federated settings
